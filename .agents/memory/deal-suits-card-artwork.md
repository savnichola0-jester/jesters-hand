name: Deal and SUITS card artwork
description: Canonical visual and interaction rule for authored Jester's Deal and SUITS cards.
---

The supplied tall, distressed black card artwork is the canonical surface for both Jester's Deal task cards and SUITS in-play cards. Hand dealers type directly inside that card; published or active cards keep the identical artwork and render the saved text read-only for members.

**Why:** This card matches the book's world. Generic forms, substitute card frames, or editor-only artwork break the intended continuity between authoring and what members receive.

**How to apply:** Any future Deal or SUITS card editor/viewer must share the same card surface. Preserve the distinction between editable draft fields for 00-00/01-54 and locked member-facing content after publish/activation.
