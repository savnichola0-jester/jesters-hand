CORE RULES
🗡️ Jester's Table is not a dating space.
Sexual content, explicit messages, or attempts to pursue romantic or sexual relationships within Jester's Table are not allowed. Please take that elsewhere or to your personal Whispers.
🗡️ Sexual or explicit content directed toward members without their consent is not allowed.
Personal Whispers are private conversations between members. However, if a member reports unwanted sexual, explicit, harassing, or inappropriate behavior within Whispers, the report will be reviewed fairly and may result in the revocation of a Joker ID and removal from The Hand.
🖤 Discussion of explicit material within the books is permitted.
🖤 Fictional characters are not protected. Language toward characters is allowed.
🗡️ Members are protected. Slurs or derogatory language toward people are not allowed.
🗡️ Real-world partisan politics and divisive social issues are not permitted within Jester's Hand.
This includes, but is not limited to, elections, political parties, politicians, current political figures, political campaigns, gun control, abortion, culture-war topics, and other real-world political or ideological debates.
These topics are not permitted within Jester's Table, The Ante, polls, debates, personal polls, discussion channels, or any other area of Jester's Hand.
Please take those discussions elsewhere.
🖤 Political dynamics, power structures, moral conflicts, and themes that exist within The Jokers may be discussed.
🖤 Cussing — filtered or unfiltered — is welcome. We are all adults.
🗡️ No bullying.
🖤 Friendly banter is allowed.
🗡️ If your personal Whispers are reported, there will be a fair investigation and it may result in removal from The Hand.
THE HAND
🗡️ Content within The Vault, Chamber, digital books, author notes, and member-only areas may not be redistributed, reposted, copied, or shared outside The Hand.
🖤 Not every situation can be covered by a written rule. The Jester reserves the right to make decisions in the best interest of The Hand and its members.
